       <dd>TreeMap 2023 CONUS provides a tree-level model of the forests of the conterminous United States. Throughout the conterminous United States (CONUS), for each forested pixel in 30×30 meter (m) gridded landscape data for circa 2023, we identified and assigned the most similar Forest Inventory and Analysis (FIA) plot.
